# Getting Started

## Prerequisites

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)

## Accessing the Application

1. Open `http://localhost` in your browser
2. Create an account using the registration form (email + password)
3. Log in with your credentials

## Using the Risk Assessment

1. After logging in, fill out the health information form with your data
2. The form includes 20 health indicators (blood pressure, BMI, smoking status, etc.)
3. Click **Estimate Risk** to get your diabetes risk assessment
4. The result shows your risk percentage and category (Low / Medium / High)

## Important Notes

- This tool is for educational and screening purposes only
- It is not a substitute for professional medical advice
- Consult a healthcare provider for proper diagnosis and treatment
